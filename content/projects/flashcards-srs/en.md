## The problem

Re-reading a whole word list every day wastes time on words you already know, but skipping review means forgetting. Research on *spaced repetition* says the best time to review is **just before you forget**.

## How to use it

1. Create a deck and **paste two columns from Excel/Google Sheets**, or type "word - meaning" per line (duplicates are skipped)
2. Press "Study" — read the front, recall, press `Space` to reveal
3. Rate yourself: `1` Again · `2` Hard · `3` Good · `4` Easy — **each button shows when you'll see the card next** (1 day / 6 days / 15 days)
4. Or switch on **multiple choice**, with distractors drawn from other cards in the deck

## Details I cared about

- Standard **SM-2** (SuperMemo), with tests for the 1 → 6 → interval × ease ladder and the 1.3 ease floor
- A daily cap on new cards so reviews don't pile up
- Cards marked "Again" are re-queued within the same session
- Stats: 7-day forecast, streak, recall rate and trouble cards
- Full keyboard shortcuts — no mouse needed
