## What it does

- Enter courses, credits and grades per term → **term GPA** and **cumulative GPAX** instantly
- **Planner:** enter a target GPAX and the credits you have left → the average you need, or a straight answer that it's out of reach
- Shows whether you're on track for 1st / 2nd-class honours (typical 3.60 / 3.25)
- GPA-by-term chart, back up / restore to a file

## Thai university rules

- A = 4, B+ = 3.5 … F = 0
- S / U / W / I / P **don't count toward the average**, but S counts as earned credit
- F counts in the average but not as earned credit
- Results are **truncated** to two decimals (3.249 → 3.24), as most universities do — never rounded up

## Privacy

Grades stay in that browser only. No account, no server.
